Polish Practice

Flash cards for the Polish alphabet and its sounds. A few minutes a day, anywhere — install it on your phone and it works offline.

Alphabet
All 32 letters, A to Ż, with the sound each one makes.
Digraphs
ch, cz, sz, rz, dz, dż, dź and szcz: several letters, one sound.
Soft sounds
ć, ś, ź, ń, dź and their ci, si, zi, ni, dzi spellings.
Sound pairs
The contrasts that trip learners up: sz or ś, i or y, ż or rz.

How it works

  • Tap a card to flip it and check the sound.
  • Swipe right (or tap Got it) if you knew it, swipe left (or tap Again) if you didn’t.
  • Cards you miss come back sooner; cards you know move to the back of the deck.
  • Say it shows a letter: say its sound. Spell it shows a word with a gap: name the missing letter.
  • Hear it reads the word aloud using your device’s Polish voice.

Your progress is saved on this device.

Install on your phone

iPhone or iPad: open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android: open the browser menu (⋮) and tap Install app or Add to Home screen.

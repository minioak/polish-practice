Alphabet

All 32 letters of the Polish alphabet. Polish spelling is very regular: learn these sounds and you can read almost any word aloud.

There is no Q, V or X in native Polish words.

Letter

Sound

How to say it

Example

A a
/a/
Open and short, like the "a" in "father" — but quicker.
mama — mum
Ą ą
/ɔ̃/
A nasal "o", like French "bon". Before b/p it sounds like "om"; at the end of a word, like a nasal "ow".
mąka — flour
B b
/b/
As in English "bed". At the end of a word it softens to "p".
babcia — grandma
C c
/t͡s/
Always "ts", like the end of "cats" — never "k" or "s".
noc — night
Ć ć
/t͡ɕ/
A soft "ch": tongue flat against the roof of your mouth, lips spread in a smile. Softer than "cheese".
ćma — moth
D d
/d/
As in English "dog", with the tongue touching the back of the teeth.
dom — house
E e
/ɛ/
Like the "e" in "bed". Always pronounced, even at the end of a word.
ser — cheese
Ę ę
/ɛ̃/
A nasal "e", like French "vin". At the end of a word it is usually just a plain "e".
ręka — hand
F f
/f/
As in English "fan".
foka — seal
G g
/ɡ/
Always hard, as in "go" — never like "gem".
gazeta — newspaper
H h
/x/
A breathy, rough sound like "ch" in Scottish "loch". Same sound as ch.
herbata — tea
I i
/i/
Like "ee" in "see", but shorter. It also softens the consonant before it.
miś — teddy bear
J j
/j/
Like "y" in "yes" — never like "jam".
jajko — egg
K k
/k/
As in English "kit".
kot — cat
L l
/l/
A clear "l", as in "leaf" — never the dark "l" of "full".
lato — summer
Ł ł
/w/
Sounds like English "w" in "wet".
łóżko — bed
M m
/m/
As in English "map".
mleko — milk
N n
/n/
As in English "net".
nos — nose
Ń ń
/ɲ/
Like "ny" in "canyon", or Spanish ñ.
koń — horse
O o
/ɔ/
Like "o" in British "hot" — short, never "oh".
okno — window
Ó ó
/u/
Exactly the same sound as u: "oo" as in "book". Just a different spelling.
góra — mountain
P p
/p/
As in English "pen", with less puff of air.
pies — dog
R r
/r/
A rolled or tapped r, like Spanish "pero" — tongue tip flicks behind the teeth.
ryba — fish
S s
/s/
Always a hiss as in "sun" — never a "z" sound.
sok — juice
Ś ś
/ɕ/
A soft "sh": tongue flat, lips smiling. Softer and higher than "sh" in "sheep".
śnieg — snow
T t
/t/
As in English "top", with the tongue touching the back of the teeth.
tak — yes
U u
/u/
Like "oo" in "book" — round your lips.
ulica — street
W w
/v/
Sounds like English "v" in "van".
woda — water
Y y
/ɨ/
Like "i" in "bit", with the tongue pulled back. Different from i!
syn — son
Z z
/z/
As in English "zoo".
zupa — soup
Ź ź
/ʑ/
A soft, buzzing "zh": ś with your voice switched on.
źle — badly
Ż ż
/ʐ/
A hard "zh", like the "s" in "pleasure" with the tongue curled back. Same sound as rz.
żaba — frog

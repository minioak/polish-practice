Digraphs

Two (or more) letters that make a single sound. Polish uses these instead of extra letters.

Letters

Sound

How to say it

Example

ch
/x/
The same sound as h: a rough breath like "ch" in Scottish "loch".
chleb — bread
cz
/t͡ʂ/
A hard "ch" as in "church", with the tongue tip raised and pulled back.
czas — time
sz
/ʂ/
A hard "sh" as in "shop", with the tongue curled slightly back.
szkoła — school
rz
/ʐ/
Exactly the same as ż — the "s" in "pleasure". After p, t, k or ch it turns into sz.
rzeka — river
dz
/d͡z/
Like "ds" in "beds", said as one sound.
dzwon — bell
dż
/d͡ʐ/
Like "j" in "jam", but harder, with the tongue curled back.
dżem — jam
dź
/d͡ʑ/
A soft "dj", like "j" in "jeep" with the tongue flat. Written dzi before a vowel.
dźwięk — sound
szcz
/ʂt͡ʂ/
sz and cz together, like the "sh ch" in "fresh cheese".
szczęście — happiness

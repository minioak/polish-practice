Soft sounds

Polish has a set of soft sounds made with the tongue flat against the roof of the mouth. Each has two spellings: with an accent (ć, ś, ź, ń, dź) or with an i (ci, si, zi, ni, dzi) before a vowel.

Letters

Sound

How to say it

Example

ć
/t͡ɕ/
A soft "ch" with the tongue flat and lips smiling. Written ć at the end of a word or before a consonant.
być — to be
ci
/t͡ɕ/
The same sound as ć. Before a vowel the i only shows softness; before a consonant say ć + i.
ciocia — aunt
ś
/ɕ/
A soft "sh" with the tongue flat and lips smiling. Written ś at the end of a word or before a consonant.
środa — Wednesday
si
/ɕ/
The same sound as ś, spelled si before a vowel.
siostra — sister
ź
/ʑ/
A soft "zh": ś with your voice on. Written ź at the end of a word or before a consonant.
późno — late
zi
/ʑ/
The same sound as ź. Before a vowel it is just ź; before a consonant say ź + i.
zima — winter
ń
/ɲ/
Like "ny" in "canyon". Written ń at the end of a word or before a consonant.
dzień — day
ni
/ɲ/
The same sound as ń, spelled ni before a vowel.
niebo — sky
dź
/d͡ʑ/
A soft "dj" with the tongue flat. Written dź at the end of a word or before a consonant.
niedźwiedź — bear
dzi
/d͡ʑ/
The same sound as dź, spelled dzi before a vowel.
dziecko — child
